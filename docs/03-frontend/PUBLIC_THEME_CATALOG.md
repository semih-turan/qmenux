# QMenuX Public Menu Theme Catalog — 10 Themes
Version: v7 | Status: Theme briefs approved; designs to be implemented and tested

Only customer-facing QR menus use these selectable themes. Admin and superadmin always use one fixed design system.

| ID | Name | Suitable for | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| coffee-minimal | Coffee Minimal | Specialty coffee / espresso bars | Cream, espresso brown, editorial type, compact list, optional images |
| botanical-cafe | Botanical Café | Cafés, brunch, patisseries | Sage, ivory, spacious cards, botanical accents |
| dark-dining | Dark Dining | Premium restaurants, steakhouses | Charcoal, copper, immersive product photography |
| street-food | Street Food | Burger, pizza, quick service | Energetic orange/red, bold type, clear price hierarchy |
| classic-bistro | Classic Bistro | Neighborhood cafés and restaurants | Timeless cream, ink, compact readable categories |
| elegant-serif | Elegant Serif | Fine dining and patisserie | Restrained typography, generous whitespace |
| mediterranean | Mediterranean | Mediterranean restaurants, seaside cafés | Sand, sea blue, sunny natural tones |
| japanese-minimal | Japanese Minimal | Sushi/Asian restaurants, tea bars | Minimal neutral palette, disciplined grid |
| modern-grid | Modern Grid | Mixed-menu chains, contemporary cafés | Modular product tiles, strong search/navigation |
| retro-diner | Retro Diner | Diner, burger, dessert bars | Vintage warmth, mustard/red accents, high legibility |

## Contract
- Exactly ten public themes in V1, selectable by every approved tenant with preview, publish, rollback; do not promise ten are already implemented.
- A theme controls layout, typography, colors, decorative style, hero and product card design; it must not change product truth, pricing, required legal labels, one-image maximum or data visibility policy.
- Themes must work with zero product images, long Turkish names, translation, allergens, sold-out status, optional Wi-Fi, WhatsApp, geo restrictions, table QR, search/filters and 320px screens.
- Do not force café menus to show burger hero photos or restaurant menus to show coffee imagery. Business-specific image and content selection comes from tenant data.
- Theme registry keys stable and versioned; accessibility tests and snapshots for every theme.
- PDF exports use their own print template, independent of public theme.
