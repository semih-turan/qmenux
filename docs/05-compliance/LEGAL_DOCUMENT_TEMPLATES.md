# Legal Document Templates

> Working compliance specification · 2026-10-25 · Requires qualified legal/accounting review before production.

## Required counsel-reviewed customer-facing templates
- `MERCHANT_SAAS_AGREEMENT`: one fixed license, period, pricing/taxes, renewal/manual grace, no implied perpetual access, permitted use, availability/support, IP, termination, export and disputes.
- `MERCHANT_ONBOARDING_NOTICE`: merchant responsibility for accurate prices, ingredients, allergens, kcal, printed menu availability, physical display and updates.
- `PLATFORM_PRIVACY_NOTICE`: identity, purposes, legal grounds, recipients, rights, retention and contact channel.
- `DATA_PROCESSING_AGREEMENT`: parties, instructions, subprocessor authorization, security, data return/deletion and transfers if applicable.
- `COOKIE_NOTICE_AND_PREFERENCES`: purpose, vendor, duration, category and withdrawal; optional cookies disabled before valid consent where required.
- `PAYMENT_AND_REFUND_POLICY`: payment types, invoicing, renewals, failed payments, refunds, chargebacks; B2B/B2C applicability reviewed.
- `AI_ACCEPTABLE_USE_AND_DISCLOSURE`: rights in uploads, generated media, representation disclaimer, human validation for nutrition/allergens, AI credit charging/refund.

## Template approval workflow
Draft -> counsel review -> approved version -> localization -> UI placement -> recorded acceptance (where appropriate) -> archive superseded versions. **These are drafting specifications, not finalized legal contract texts.**
